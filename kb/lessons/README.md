# Lessons from real projects

What earlier projects learned about Blender 4.5.14 by running it for weeks: a cloth garment draped on an animated figure, rigged figures, hair, look renders and asset export, in batches on a 16 GB Apple Silicon laptop. Each lesson says what happens, the numbers measured, what to do, and how it was found. The projects are described, not named.

A lesson marked *re-checked here* was run again in this repository (`experiments/`), *not reproduced* means a simple test here came out the other way, and *not re-checked here* means it rests on the project's own measurement. The area pages in `skills/blender/areas/` carry the lessons that matter most, tagged `[lesson]`.
