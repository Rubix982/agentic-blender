# The photograph

`agra-jali.jpg`: "Window screen (jali), northern India, Agra area, 1630-50; Louvre Abu Dhabi (2)", by Prof. Mortel,
taken 12 March 2022, <https://www.flickr.com/photos/43714545@N06/51973919457/>, licensed
[CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). The file is the original as published on Flickr (2460 x 2803,
Samsung SM-G998B main camera, 24 mm equivalent, edited in Polarr), unchanged.

The screen is carved and pierced red sandstone. The museum's own record was not found online, so its size here is an
estimate from comparable Agra-area screens of the same years: 124 x 105 x 6.1 cm (Khalili Collection MXD 260, c.
1630-40) and 124.5 x 102 x 7 cm (an Asian Art in London 2001 exhibit, c. 1630-40).

`c021_sibling_vs_ours.jpg`: a crop of the flower under the arch's crown from two photos, left from "Window screen (jali),
northern India, Agra area, 1630-50; Louvre Abu Dhabi (1)" <https://www.flickr.com/photos/43714545@N06/51973919577/>,
right from ours, both by Prof. Mortel, [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/); cropped and resized
(C-021). It shows that photo (1) is another screen: notched petals against round ones, no white specks on its stone.
